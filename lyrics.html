SAHIBA

> Sahiba, aaye ghar kaahe na?
Aise toh sataye na Dekhun tujhko,
chain aata hai Sahiba,
neendein-veendein aayein na
Raatein kaati jaayein na
Tera hi khayaal din-rain aata hai Sahiba,
samundar meri aankhon
mein reh gaye Hum aate-aate,
jaana, teri yaadon mein reh gaye
Yeh palkein gawaahi hain,

hum raaton mein reh gaye
Jo waade kiye saare bas
baaton mein reh gaye Baaton-
baaton mein hi, khwabon-
khwabon mein hi
Mere qareeb hai tu
Teri talab mujhko,
teri talab, jaana Ho
tu kabhi roo-ba-roo
Shor-sharaba jo seene
mein hai mere
